## Fixed: directional ground jumps played weird clips in every direction but forward

**Where:** `Scripts/Core/AnimationIds.cs`, `Scripts/Player/Animation/Movement/PlayerAnimator.cs`,
`Scripts/Player/Data/PlayerController.cs` (+ cref/comment fixes in `GlyphCastVariant.cs`,
`LocomotionGraph.cs`, `tools/test_animation_clips.gd`).

**Symptom:** each jump direction selected its own synthesized clip (4 families × 2 lead
feet), and everything except the forward family read as broken rather than as a jump.

**Decision (user):** use the forward jump animation for *all* directions and show the
direction as a slight tilt of the model instead.

**Fix:**
- `AnimationIds.JumpClipFor` now takes only the lead foot and resolves `Jump Forward L/R`;
  `JumpFamilyFor` and the six non-forward jump constants are gone, and those six clips left
  `ReachableClips` (18 states / 306 transitions now, down from 24/552). The clips are still
  generated into the library — only the graph states went away, so bringing them back is a
  constant list edit.
- `PlayerAnimator` picks `JumpClipFor(lead foot)` whenever a jump is latched; the lead-foot
  alternation (gait-derived on the ground jump, alternating through the air-jump chain) is
  untouched, as is the bare `Jumping Up` clip for standing jumps and ledge falls.
- `PlayerController` gains `_jumpTilt` — the fired input clamped to unit length — latched
  with the jump, swung to the held input on a successful air jump (a neutral air jump keeps
  the arc's own lean), cleared on any grounded frame without a jump. `UpdateLean` now has an
  airborne branch: pitch/roll the visual `JumpTiltDeg` (12°, exported) toward the tilt at
  `JumpTiltSpeed` (90°/s, exported), sign convention matching the swim lean. A fall has no
  latch and stays level.

**Verification:** `tools/run_tests.sh` → **pass=19 fail=1 crash=0 silent=3**, byte-identical
to the 10-05 baseline: the one failure is the pre-existing `test_stickman` waterline tread,

## Fixed: the double-jump air-step cast still played directional leg drives

**Where:** `Scripts/Player/Data/PlayerController.cs` (`FireAirStepCast`), plus comment fixes in
`Scripts/Glyphs/Framework/GlyphCastVariant.cs` and `Scripts/Core/AnimationIds.cs`.

**Symptom (user):** "the knees and legs are flopping all over the place" on directional jumps;
every direction should drive the legs up in front like the straight-up/forward clip, with the
tilt being the only difference.

**Cause:** Session 1 collapsed the *ground-jump* clips to the forward family, but the double
jump's cast was untouched: `FireAirStepCast` still keyed the step by the held direction
(`GlyphCastVariant.KeyFor(direction, leadsLeft)`), so a strafe double jump played the
synthesized `Air Step Strafe L/R`, which abducts the leg shape sideways (`_leap_hip` about the
back axis in `tools/build_stickman.gd`), and the backward one drove the legs behind — the flop.

**Fix:** `FireAirStepCast` no longer takes input; every air step plays `KeyFor(Forward, lead
foot)`, same collapse as the ground jump. Direction still reaches the body as the tilt — the
air-jump block that calls it already swings `_jumpTilt` toward the held input. The lead-foot
alternation across the chain is untouched, and the `PlayerAnimator.Frame` no longer receives
the input (only used by `PredictAirTime`, which reads the vertical velocity and ground gap).
The six non-forward cast variants and their clips stay in `DoubleJump.tres`/`AirStep.tscn`
(selecting them is a one-line change per call site); `GlyphCastVariant.KeyFor` itself is
unchanged and still serving casts that genuinely aim by direction.

**Verification:** `tools/run_tests.sh` → **pass=19 fail=1 crash=0 silent=3**, identical to
baseline; `test_air_jump_animation` fires both air jumps with no input and still expects `Air
Step Forward L` then the alternate lead foot, so it passes unchanged — the directional air-step
selection has no test coverage, and the strafe/backward demonstrates were never preserved.

**Docs:** `docs/design/PlayerDecisions.md` — the 10-06 tilt bullet now covers every jump (ground
