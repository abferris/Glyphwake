# Session 1 — the chamber fires faster: coil and push pulled to the front of the step

## Todo status

`docs/future/CURRENT_TODO.md` unchanged — animation retuning of the air-step clips; no todo
item was completed or newly outstanding.

## What changed

**Where:** `tools/build_stickman.gd` (the `ASTEP_*` beat constants), `Scenes/AirStep.tscn`
(regenerated), `docs/design/PlayerDecisions.md`.

The user's read on session 12's clips: "The chambered knee before jumping looks better, but
needs to happen faster." The coil was **correct but slow**: `ASTEP_COIL_START` 0.15 with
`ASTEP_COIL_SPAN` 0.40 meant the pelvis bottomed out at phase **0.333** — a full third of the
step spent loading before anything pushed. Sampled off the clip, the pelvis fell 0.8156 →
0.6394 by phase 0.333 and only then began rising.

A jump chambers and fires; it does not load, hold and release. Three boundaries moved so the
chamber completes inside the front fifth and the push follows immediately:

| constant | was | now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ASTEP_COIL_START` | 0.15 | **0.02** |
| `ASTEP_COIL_SPAN` | 0.40 | **0.20** |
| `ASTEP_PUSH_START` | 0.45 | **0.28** |
| `ASTEP_PUSH_SPAN` | 0.35 | 0.32 |
| `ASTEP_DESCENT_SPAN` | 0.60 | 0.58 |
| `ASTEP_LEAD_PEAK` | 0.35 | **0.20** |

`ASTEP_LEAD_PEAK` came along because with the coil that early, a lead knee still peaking at
0.35 read as a separate, slower beat instead of driving with the jump. It now matches the
chamber's speed.

**Why the chain continuity survived.** Every envelope in `_astep_beats` saturates before the
clip ends (`coil` is a bell, `down`/`push`/`rise` are monotonic, `settle` returns to 0), so the
clip's end pose — the pose the next step chains from — is independent of where the beats sit.
That was checked rather than assumed: see the CHAIN line below.

# Session 2 — the push-off fires: faster and with a drive-through overshoot

## Todo status

`docs/future/CURRENT_TODO.md` unchanged — another air-step clip retune; no todo item was
completed or newly outstanding.

## The read

The user's ask, on the double jump: "we have a knee driving up, and one leg pushing down. I want
the pushing down leg to go faster with more power." The plant leg was the slow half of the step.
Session 1 had pulled the coil to the front but left the *extension* on the old clock:
`ASTEP_DESCENT_SPAN` 0.58 and `ASTEP_PUSH_START` 0.28 with `ASTEP_PUSH_SPAN` 0.32 meant the
thigh took 0.60 of the step to come down and the knee only began firing at 0.28 — so the push
leg was still arriving at full extension while the clip was more than half spent, and it stopped
*dead* there. Dead-stop at extension reads as reaching a pose, not as throwing off the ground.

## What changed

**Where:** `tools/build_stickman.gd` (`_astep_beats`, `_astep_offset`, the `ASTEP_*` block),
`Scenes/AirStep.tscn` (regenerated), `docs/design/PlayerDecisions.md`.

Two things, in the builder's own idiom rather than as a runtime multiplier — the clip is scaled
to the arc it flies at runtime, so speed is authored into the beats.

**1. The extension moved to the front and compressed** — same amount of travel, far less time:

| constant | was | now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ASTEP_DESCENT_SPAN` | 0.58 | **0.30** (thigh vertical by phase 0.32) |
| `ASTEP_PUSH_START` | 0.28 | **0.14** |
| `ASTEP_PUSH_SPAN` | 0.32 | **0.20** (knee straight by phase 0.34) |
| `ASTEP_ROOT_RISE` | 0.15 | **0.22** (a harder push throws the body higher) |
| `ASTEP_SETTLE_SPAN` | `1.0 - PUSH_START` | **0.55** (named, so the pelvis/arm bell is a tunable span rather than "the rest of the clip") |

**2. A new `drive` envelope that overshoots.** `_astep_beats` gained a sixth bell, and the plant
leg's three bones use it past full extension (`ASTEP_DRIVE_DEG` 26° of hip extension past
vertical, `ASTEP_DRIVE_KNEE_DEG` 8° of knee snap-through, `ASTEP_DRIVE_TOE_DEG` 22° of toe
through flat). Measured off the envelopes, the plant knee now travels 124.7° (peak chamber,
phase 0.13) → −7.9° (drive peak, phase 0.39) in 0.26 of the step, peaking at **81°/s** of knee
angular rate around phase 0.22, against a peak of 18°/s during the chamber.

**The drive is a bell that returns, and that is load-bearing, not cosmetic.** First attempt held
it: `sin(PI * 0.5 * …)`, monotonic, saturating like every other envelope in the file — which
broke the chain to **0.28272 m**. The clips chain by swapping legs, so this step's plant leg *is*
the next step's lead leg: the clip's end pose is the next clip's start pose bone for bone, and a
held hip-extension overshoot ends the plant leg in a pose no lead leg starts in. That is the one
invariant here that "every envelope saturates" did *not* cover — saturation alone was not enough,
the envelope has to come back. `sin(PI * pow(t, ASTEP_DRIVE_BIAS))` with `ASTEP_DRIVE_BIAS` 0.35
puts the peak at phase 0.39 (snap out in 0.09 of the step) and the return at 0.83, so the snap is
faster than the relax.
